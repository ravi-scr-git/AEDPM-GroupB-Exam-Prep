# 🚂 Indian Railways AEDPM (Group 'B') Exam Preparation Portal & CBT Simulator

An enterprise-grade, offline-capable study portal and interactive Computer Based Test (CBT) exam simulator developed specifically for the **Assistant Electronic Data Processing Manager (AEDPM)** Group 'B' selection (CBT/TBT Vacancy Cycle 2027–2028), based on Railway Board Notification No. `E(GP)2026/2/5` and official railway references.

The top of the left sidebar features a 4-tab filter bar:

## 📚 Encyclopedic Content & Statutory Analysis

### 1. Establishment Rules (Page 2 — 11 Topics)
- **Statutory Citations:** Indian Railway Establishment Code (IREC) Vols. I & II, Indian Railway Establishment Manual (IREM) Vols. I & II, RS (Conduct) Rules 1966, RS (D&A) Rules 1968.
- **Standard Forms (SF-1 to SF-11):** Detailed statutory procedures for Minor Penalties (SF-11) and Major Penalties (SF-5), Inquiry Officer appointment (SF-7), and Presenting Officer (SF-8).
- **Consolidation of 29 Labour Laws:** Detailed analysis of the **4 New Labour Codes, 2020**:
  - Code on Wages, 2019
  - Industrial Relations Code, 2020
  - Code on Social Security, 2020
  - Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions (OSH) Code, 2020
- **Modern Pension Schemes:** Transition from OPS & NPS to the **Unified Pension Scheme (UPS)** guaranteeing 50% assured pension and Dearness Relief indexation.
- **Digital Railway Administration:** CRIS HRMS (e-SR, online Leave, digital e-Pass/PTO with UPN), SPARROW APAR, paperless e-Office, ARPAN, and CPGRAMS.

### 2. Financial Rules (Page 3 — 17 Topics)
- **Constitutional Framework:** Articles 112–117, 265, 266, and 148–151.
- **Canons of Financial Propriety:** Verbatim text and application of **Para 116 of IRFC Vol. I** (Prudent man rule, no self-advantage, no personal benefit, allowance bounds).
- **Budgetary Cycle:** Demand No. 85, August Review, Revised Estimates (RE), Final Modification (FM), Exchequer Control (Cash Management System).
- **Capital Works & Justification:** Works Programme, Pink Book, Rate of Return (ROR $\ge 10\%$) under Discounted Cash Flow (DCF), General Conditions of Contract (GCC 2022).
- **Procurement & Stores:** Stores Code Vol. I, Inventory Control, ABC & VED Analysis, Government e-Marketplace (GeM 4.0), IREPS e-Tendering, Model SOP 2018.

## 💻 CBT Simulator Rules & Scoring
- **100 randomized questions** drawn across all domains.
- **120-minute timer** with auto-submission upon timeout.
- **Railway Board Marking Scheme:**
  - **+1.0 Mark** for correct answer.
  - **-1/3 (-0.33) Mark** deduction for incorrect answer.
  - **0.0 Marks** for unattempted questions.
- **Qualifying Standard:** 60% aggregate score (60 marks out of 100).
